# Report pages and business questions

The Power BI report contains three pages: **Sales Dashboard**, **Products**, and **Customers**. The topics below are grounded in the page names and the measures and fields referenced by their report visuals.

## 1. Sales Dashboard

**Purpose:** Provide an at-a-glance view of sales activity and profitability.

**Questions answered**
- How are total sales, profit, and order volume performing?
- How do sales, profit, orders, and average order value compare with the previous year?
- How does profitability vary across markets?
- Which periods and geographic areas contribute to overall performance?

**Relevant metrics and fields:** Total Sales, Profit, Profit Margin %, Total Orders, Average Order Value, Sales YoY %, Profit YoY %, Orders YoY %, AOV YoY %, Date, and Sales Territory / Country.

## 2. Products

**Purpose:** Understand the contribution of products and product groups to sales performance.

**Questions answered**
- Which products, categories, and subcategories contribute the most sales?
- How much quantity is sold?
- How does average selling price compare with the previous year?
- How do product sales and profit margins differ across selections?

**Relevant metrics and fields:** Total Sales, Profit, Profit Margin %, Quantity Sold, Average Selling Price, Quantity Sold YoY %, Average Selling Price YoY %, Product, Category, and Subcategory.

## 3. Customers

**Purpose:** Explore customer purchasing behavior and commercial performance.

**Questions answered**
- How many customers contribute to sales?
- How does sales per customer compare with the previous year?
- What proportion of customers qualify as repeat customers under the report's calculation?
- How do sales and customer activity differ across channels and territories?

**Relevant metrics and fields:** Total Customers, Customer Sales, Customer Orders, Customer AOV, Sales per Customer, Repeat Customer Rate, Customers YoY %, Sales per Customer YoY %, Channel, Customer, Country, and Sales Territory.

## Navigation and interactivity

The report definition references Date fields including Fiscal Year Display and Month Display, geographic fields, and page-specific product and customer dimensions. Exact slicer interactions and cross-filter behavior should be checked in Power BI Desktop before documenting them as guaranteed.
